## Cause and repair

The prior scalar filter excluded only unread writes. Logging reads retained
unrelated scalar storage in the 256-slot summary. Member names were also shared
across unrelated aggregates. Separately, incomplete indexed helpers and expanded
call-site effect overflow marked all pointer arguments opaque, invalidating
unused callback objects.

Collect a scalar dependency closure rooted in selectors, guards and storage
connections; follow assignment/alias/parameter/return links. Identify members
by compatible aggregate identity or scoped declaration identity. Filter proven
unrelated scalar effects/regions while keeping unresolved layouts and unions.
Represent limited helper effects by their tracked/omitted storage regions
instead of blanket invalidation of every argument. Budgets remain unchanged.
