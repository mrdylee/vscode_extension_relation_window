## Design and scope

1. Annotate unobserved indirect calls when propagation stops before convergence.
   Preserve the original raw call and project notices; provide no false target.
2. Reuse the engine over a bounded small-body projection after time/work stops.
   Export additional targets solely as incomplete Registered candidates. Preserve
   the original unresolved call and all project diagnostics. Never mark a reduced
   projection as complete or replace the main engine results.
3. Keep full-analysis budgets; cooperative projection deadline 250 ms, at most
   32768 syntax containers examined, at most 512 per body/initializer. Omit large
   bodies without implying they have no effects. Skip recovery under memory
   pressure and for disabled callback analysis. Cache projection identity.
